7. DISTRIBUTION PATH REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================
FRESH MARKET / RETAIL:
- Full FDA-compliant label (all elements above)
- UPC barcode
- Vendor approval + food safety audit
- Third-party lab test (nutrition panel)
- Commercial kitchen / permitted facility
- Product liability insurance (typical ask)
- COOL (Country of Origin Labeling) if imported ingredients

FLORIDA COTTAGE FOOD LAW (home-based, no commercial kitchen):
- Cap: $250,000/year
- WHOLESALE BANNED — cannot sell to Fresh Market or any
  distributor under cottage rules
- Direct-to-consumer only (farmers markets, online, delivery)
- Label still needs: name/address, net weight, ingredients,
  allergens, "Made in a home kitchen that is not subject to
  state licensure or inspection" statement
- Not sufficient for grocery retail

PATH TO FRESH MARKET:
1. Rent permitted commercial kitchen (shared-use)
2. Lab-verify nutrition panel
3. Design + print FDA-compliant labels (Blue Label/Teal)
4. Source UPC (GS1 US, ~$30-50 first year for small biz)
5. Submit vendor packet: label mockup, lab report,
   insurance cert, kitchen permit
6. Fresh Market QA review (typically 4-8 weeks)
